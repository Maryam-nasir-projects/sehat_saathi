Important health information

Emergency Help

If someone may be in immediate danger, seek emergency medical help now. Do not wait for an appointment or online consultation.

1. Contact local emergency services

Use a verified local emergency number or ambulance service. If you do not know the number, ask someone nearby to contact emergency services immediately.

2. Contact a hospital directly

Use the official hospital websites listed above to find current contact information and emergency instructions.

View hospital websites →

3. Do not rely on this website

Sehat Saathi does not dispatch ambulances, monitor patients, provide medical diagnoses or guarantee hospital availability.

This information is general guidance, not a substitute for emergency services or professional medical advice.
